## II. Project Summary (200 words, paste-ready)

This project encompasses a 245,000-word literary-historical novel, *SGT George Ramos: The Mathematics of Vietnam*, and the forensic research infrastructure built to authorize its claims. The novel follows Chicano and non-citizen U.S. Army veterans through Vietnam-era service, post-war displacement, and deportation. It is instrumented by LITCENTRAL, a chapter-level analytics dashboard (43 chapters, behavioral vector scores, Omega composite scoring, restraint metrics, code-switch intensity), and TruthEngine360, a deportation-cycle intelligence database that integrates PSID survey variables, Pentagon DCAS casualty records, FOIA documents, and case files for fifteen verified deported veterans (MNV-01–MNV-15).

Applying Bayesian Improved Surname Geocoding (BISG) to the Defense Casualty Analysis System (DCAS), the project documents an estimated 84.9% Hispanic casualty classification failure across 58,220 Vietnam-era records. PSID logistic regression models the benefits-denial consequences for draft-eligible non-citizen veterans across six survey waves. The result is a hybrid research-and-literary project that uses the government's own databases to stress-test historical claims and present them to a general audience through narrative.

Grant support is requested for manuscript editing and production, PSID restricted data access, FOIA filing costs, archival research travel, and open-access digital publication.

## III. Statement of Need

Chicano and non-citizen Vietnam veterans exist at the intersection of three documented gaps in American institutional memory:

**1. The casualty classification gap.** Pentagon DCAS records code only 349 of 58,220 U.S. Vietnam casualties as Hispanic (0.6%). BISG surname-geocode correction produces an estimate of 2,309–3,000 (5.5–7.2%), implying an 84.9% misclassification rate. This is not a scholarly dispute — it is a finding derived from the government's own preferred methodology applied to the government's own database.

**2. The benefits denial gap.** PSID variables across six survey waves document benefits denied to household heads due to non-citizenship status. For draft-eligible, non-citizen, Vietnam-era male heads, the preliminary variable registry indicates higher denial rates than for comparable citizen veterans. The logistic regression model (`ER32054` draft category × `ER11972` naturalization × six benefits-denial waves) has not yet been run at scale because it requires PSID restricted data access — which this grant partially funds.

**3. The deportation documentation gap.** Fifteen cases (MNV-01–MNV-15) have been documented at CB-HSIVF verification tiers T1–T4. At least one pre-dates the 1996 IIRIRA (MNV-04, Manuel Mike Segura, deported 1985, died Tijuana). Current VA policy changes (July 2025 XC-file update) and contradictions in federal statements about veteran deportations (Noem September vs. December 2025) are documented in TruthEngine360's AI Search Log with source attribution.

No existing published work combines literary form with this level of forensic verification infrastructure. The gap this project fills is methodological as much as narrative: it demonstrates how community researchers can use publicly available federal data to recover veterans their own government erased.

## IV. Project Description

### A. The Manuscript

*SGT George Ramos: The Mathematics of Vietnam* is a 245,000-word literary-historical novel in four acts. Forty-three chapters are organized by tier (21 Omega Elite, remainder Gold/Silver) based on composite scoring across six behavioral dimensions: Agency, Risk, Compassion, Alignment, Code-switch, and Spiritual. The novel moves between Vietnam combat, stateside military life, barrio community, and post-war displacement, following a small cohort of Chicano and non-citizen soldiers whose service is underdocumented, whose benefits are denied, and whose citizenship — assumed by community and earned through sacrifice — is legally precarious.

The prose is bilingual (English/Spanish/Vietnamese code-switching), grounded in military technical register, and structured around the concept of "fragmented truth": that when no single official document records a community's history, the fragments — oral testimony, letters, FOIA-released files, casualty database anomalies — must be assembled into a provisional record honest about its own incompleteness.

### B. LITCENTRAL Analytics Dashboard

LITCENTRAL is a chapter-level manuscript analytics system built from scratch for this project. It computes:
- Behavioral vector scores (Agency, Risk, Compassion, Alignment, Code-switch, Spiritual) — Cronbach's α = 0.938 across pilot (N=11)
- Omega composite score: Ω = 71.443 + 0.124(CLS) + 0.118(BIS) + 0.089(SII) + 0.067(MRF), R² = 0.947
- AutoCrit benchmarks: Overall 88.3, Strong Writing 100.0, Dialogue 93.8, Repetition 77.6
- CL-MoE mean (0.115), CME_mainstream (0.435)
- Restraint scoring, code-switch frequency, register shift detection

This infrastructure ensures that revision decisions are data-informed and that the manuscript's literary quality is measurable against external benchmarks.

### C. TruthEngine360 Research Engine

TruthEngine360 is a deportation-cycle intelligence database that maps nine deportation stages (trigger enforcement through post-removal readmission) against PSID variables, FOIA targets, and individual case evidence. It includes:
- PSID Variable Registry (immigration and benefits variables, labeled by deportation-cycle stage)
- AI Search Log (structured research queries with source, status, and PSID/case crosswalk)
- Veteran Case Registry (MNV-01–MNV-15, CB-HSIVF T1–T4 verification tiers)
- DCAS Audit Results (BISG methodology, surname extraction, classification failure analysis)

## V. Methodology

**Literary methodology:** Forensic literary historiography — using archival, quantitative, and testimonial sources to authorize narrative claims, with LITCENTRAL as the quality control and scoring infrastructure.

**Quantitative methodology:** BISG surname-geocode correction (RAND RR-1162 protocol) applied to DCAS; PSID logistic regression (`ER32054` × `ER11972` × benefits-denial wave variables); multi-source case verification (CB-HSIVF T1–T4).

**Archival methodology:** FOIA requests, NARA consultation, VA BIRLS lookup, cross-reference against CMOHS, The Wall USA, and Coffelt Database.

**Transparency standard:** All claims categorized by verification tier. Claims that cannot be verified due to classification, destruction, or bureaucratic erasure are explicitly marked as unverified in the manuscript and research appendices.

---

## VI. Significance

**For literary studies:** Demonstrates that quantitative analytics (behavioral vectors, restraint scoring, CL-MoE) can be integrated into literary revision without constraining creative voice — a new model for evidence-based literary production.

**For Chicano/Latino studies:** Provides the first BISG-corrected analysis of Chicano Vietnam casualty undercounting and links it empirically to post-war benefits denial and deportation.

**For veterans' policy:** Documents the pre-IIRIRA deportation pattern (MNV-04, 1985) and links current deportation practices to measurable PSID outcomes, creating a policy-relevant evidence base.

**For digital humanities:** The LITCENTRAL + TruthEngine360 architecture is a replicable model for community researchers who lack institutional archive access but can use federal public data.

**For intergenerational memory:** As stated in the project's core framing: *the history was only implicit and obscure at best; now our children and grandchildren will not even see it coming, because they do not exist in the narrative.* This project exists to place them in the record.
