# Legal Argument Brief: Proof of Concept Through Government Data Stress-Testing
## *SGT George Ramos: The Mathematics of Vietnam*

---

## I. Core Legal Argument

The central argument is this: **when a researcher uses a government's own databases, official casualty records, FOIA-disclosed documents, and federally administered survey instruments to validate claims embedded in a literary work, that work acquires evidentiary standing that transcends conventional fiction.**

This is not a claim that the novel is a court document. It is the narrower, more defensible claim that:

1. The factual predicate of the work was stress-tested against sources the federal government itself recognizes as authoritative;
2. Every claim that can be verified was verified, and every claim that cannot be verified due to classification, destruction, or bureaucratic erasure was honestly represented as such;
3. The methodology used to construct the evidentiary layer (BISG surname geocoding, PSID variable extraction, DCAS surname audit, FOIA cross-reference, BIRLS lookup) is the same methodology used in peer-reviewed policy research and federal program evaluation;
4. Therefore, the government's own data — the Defense Casualty Analysis System (DCAS), the Panel Study of Income Dynamics (PSID), NARA records, EOIR deportation files, VA benefits adjudication records — either confirms, supports, or is structurally silent on the claims made in the manuscript;
5. Structural silence in a federal database is itself evidentiary: it documents the erasure, not the absence.

## II. The Government Database Argument (Detailed)

### A. DCAS — Pentagon Defense Casualty Analysis System

The U.S. Department of Defense maintains DCAS as the authoritative record of Vietnam-era casualties. DCAS codes each casualty by race/ethnicity based on self-reported or administratively assigned categories.

Application to this project:
- The manuscript applies Bayesian Improved Surname Geocoding (BISG) — the same methodology used by RAND Corporation (RR-1162) and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — to the DCAS surname field across all 58,220 U.S. casualties.
- Result: an estimated 2,309–3,000 Hispanic casualties vs. 349 officially coded, representing an 84.9% classification failure rate.
- This is not the author's claim against the government. **This is the government's database, audited by the government's own preferred methodology, producing a result the government's own database cannot refute.**

**Legal significance:** The BISG methodology has been upheld as reliable in federal fair-lending enforcement and CFPB rulemaking. Its application to DCAS produces findings that are methodologically defensible in an academic, regulatory, or legislative context.

### B. PSID — Panel Study of Income Dynamics (University of Michigan / NORC)

PSID is the world's longest-running longitudinal household survey, funded in part by federal agencies. The project uses restricted PSID variables, specifically:

- `ER32054` — Vietnam Draft Category (restricted variable)
- `ER11972` — Naturalization status
- `ER16235`, `ER16270`, `ER20181`, `ER20216`, `ER23782`, `ER23817` — Benefits denied due to non-citizenship status (six survey waves)
- `ER57652` — First year in U.S.
- `ER11928` — Children outside U.S.

The planned logistic regression: draft-eligible non-citizen male heads (PSID Category 1) against benefits denial rates, controlling for service era and household composition.

**Legal significance:** PSID data is a federal resource. Using it to model the economic consequences of deporting draft-eligible veterans is research, not advocacy. The findings, if they confirm higher benefits-denial rates for non-citizen veterans, constitute independent corroboration of the manuscript's historical claims using a federally funded instrument.

### C. FOIA-Disclosed Records

FOIA requests filed against VA, USCIS, DHS, and DOJ have produced, or are pending production of, records relating to:
- VA XC-file policy changes (July 2025)
- Deportation proceedings against individuals with documented military service
- Benefits adjudication outcomes for non-citizen veterans

**Legal significance:** FOIA disclosure is the federal government's own transparency mechanism. Documents produced through FOIA carry the government's implicit authentication. Citing them in the manuscript's evidentiary layer is not editorializing — it is using the government's own process to hold the government's own record accountable.

### D. CMOHS, The Wall USA, Coffelt Database, BIRLS

These databases — the Congressional Medal of Honor Society, the Vietnam Veterans Memorial Fund's online name registry, the Coffelt Veterans Database, and VA's Beneficiary Identification and Records Locator Subsystem — collectively represent the institutional memory of Vietnam service. Cross-referencing the manuscript's fifteen named cases (MNV-01 through MNV-15) against all four produces a multi-source verification chain that no single database could provide.

**Legal significance:** Multi-source corroboration is the standard for both historical scholarship and evidentiary reliability. Each database is maintained by an institution (federal agency, veterans' organization, or congressionally chartered body) that carries its own authority.

## III. The "Proof of Concept" Frame

A proof of concept, in both legal and scientific contexts, is a demonstration that a hypothesis is realizable — not that it has been proven beyond doubt, but that it is empirically testable, the methodology is sound, and the preliminary results are non-trivial.

This project presents:

1. **A testable hypothesis**: That Chicano and non-citizen Vietnam veterans were systematically undercounted in federal records, denied benefits based on immigration status, and subsequently deported in violation of both the moral and legal expectations created by their military service.

2. **A sound methodology**: BISG, PSID regression modeling, multi-source case verification (CB-HSIVF T1–T4), and FOIA cross-reference — each of which has independent academic and regulatory standing.

3. **Non-trivial preliminary results**: The DCAS 84.9% classification failure, the six PSID waves of benefits denial variables, fifteen documented cases across four verification tiers, and at least one documented pre-IIRIRA deportation (MNV-04, Manuel Mike Segura, 1985) that predates the 1996 law most scholarship treats as the origin of the deportation problem.

4. **A literary vehicle**: The manuscript is not the proof. The manuscript is the medium through which a general audience can encounter the proof. The evidentiary layer (LITCENTRAL, TruthEngine360) authorizes the narrative claims; the narrative makes those claims human.

**Summary argument for institutional presentation:**

> The government's own databases — maintained at federal expense, validated by federal methodology, and subject to federal FOIA disclosure — either confirm the claims in this manuscript or document the administrative silence that makes confirmation impossible. Either result is evidentiary. The project does not ask institutions to take the author's word. It asks them to look at their own data.

## IV. Potential Objections and Responses

**Objection 1: "This is fiction. Fiction has no evidentiary standing."\
Response:** The evidentiary layer (LITCENTRAL, TruthEngine360, DCAS audit) is non-fiction scholarship. The novel is the delivery mechanism, not the claim. The project is structured so the scholarship can stand independently of the literary form.

**Objection 2: "BISG is a probabilistic method, not a definitive identification."\
Response:** Correct. BISG produces population-level estimates, not individual identifications. The project explicitly represents estimates as estimates, with confidence intervals, and uses language like "2,309–3,000 (5.5–7.2%)" rather than "exactly 2,309." This is methodologically honest and consistent with how BISG is used in federal fair-lending enforcement.

**Objection 3: "PSID data is restricted. You can't publish it."\
Response:** PSID restricted data is accessible under a data use agreement. Findings derived from restricted data can be published as modeled outputs, not raw records. This is standard practice in social science research.

**Objection 4: "Fifteen cases is too small a sample."\
Response:** The fifteen documented cases (MNV-01–MNV-15) are not claimed to be a representative sample. They are an existence proof — they demonstrate that the phenomenon is real, documented, and verifiable. The PSID regression provides the population-level inference; the cases provide the human evidence.
