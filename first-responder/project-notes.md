## Logline

During the COVID-19 pandemic, a long-haul trucker navigating a locked-down America finds that the recovery fellowship he’s built his life on — folding chairs, coffee, shared rooms — has gone silent. What he carries forward on empty highways is older than any program: his father’s toolbox, the road’s own language, and the obligation to stop for the man on the shoulder.

## Connection to the Canon

George Ramos was first written as a character in First Responder — a name in a squad, one scene. The character demanded his own story. That refusal to stay in a scene became 245,000 words, a forensic evidentiary infrastructure, and the Mathematics of Vietnam.

First Responder and SGT Ramos are not competing projects. They are two works from the same creative event.
