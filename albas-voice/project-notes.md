## Origin

Alba Terminel de Arce died April 6, 2023. Her son sat down to write 30 pages for her. He wrote 400,000 words.

The manuscript uncovered Francisco Terminel — Gabriel’s ancestor — as a figure in the Porfirian exploitation of Yaqui communal lands in Sonora. The family read the manuscript and was shaken by what the research revealed. Gabriel validated their concern and kept going.

This book is too important to rush. Gabriel is taking his time with her.

---

## What It Is

A multigenerational saga beginning with Nela (María Manuela Terminel) giving birth to Alba in a cramped pantry beside a hotel kitchen in Hermosillo, Sonora, 1946. The desert pressing down. A German-engineered air conditioner. The chandelier trembling in the lobby as if touched by presence beyond sight.

Chapter 1: *Caja de Zapatos* — Shoebox.
