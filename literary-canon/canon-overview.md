## HOW THEY CONNECT

### The Origin
Alba Terminel de Arce died April 6, 2023. Gabriel was injured. He sat down to write 30 pages for his mother. He wrote 400,000 words.

His family read the manuscript. They got scared — the research had uncovered Francisco Terminel, their ancestor, as a figure in the Porfirian exploitation of Yaqui communal lands in Sonora. The family's DNA traces to Yaqui lineage through Avainia Sagasta, who married Francisco Terminel in San Javier, Sonora. Gabriel shares Yaqui genetic coding.

He validated his family's concern. He kept researching. He discovered the full arc.

### The Pivot
The Alba manuscript was too large and too close to publish yet. Medical problems slowed everything. Gabriel started **First Responder** — a shorter, more commercial work. A COVID-era novel about a truck driver, recovery, and essential workers during the pandemic.

In one scene, to fill out a squad, Gabriel wrote in a character: **George Ramos**. A real friend. A veteran. An A-8.

George Ramos refused to stay in a scene.

### The Manuscript That Wouldn’t Let Go
First Responder has 25 chapters done. George Ramos has 245,000 words and a forensic evidentiary infrastructure spanning four federal databases. His wife left. He kept writing. He built LITCENTRAL, TruthEngine360, the DCAS audit, the PSID variable registry, fifteen case files. He built a nonprofit in his mother’s name.

Here we are.
